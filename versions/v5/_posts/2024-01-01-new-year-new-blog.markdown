Well, it's 2024.

I have decided to create this blog so that, in one year's time (or rather, 364 days' time, on New Years Eve), I can look back and see everything that I have (hopefully) been able to accomplish within this year.

I can't promise that anything *will* happen, but something I've come to realize -- at long last -- is that life moves fast, and I can't afford to sit around waiting for things to happen anymore. So, let this be my commitment to 2024: the year where I start doing the things that I want to do.

See you in a year!
